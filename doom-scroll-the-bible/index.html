Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026 · Doom Scroll the Bible

Doom Scroll the Bible is an offline first Bible reader. The complete reader works without an account. If you choose Doom Scroll Plus, an account and developer operated cloud services provide optional synchronization, daily devotions, verse insights, on demand devotions, and completed sermon note sync. Sermon drafts, search, weekly reflection, export, and the Scripture widget work from local data. The app has no advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Reading without an account

The Bible text, fonts, reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes, drafts, sermon notes, sermon review responses, weekly reflections, local search, widget choices, appearance choices, and any legacy guide progress retained from an earlier version are stored on your device. Ordinary offline reading and local search do not send this reading data or search text to the developer.

You do not need an account or a subscription to read Scripture, save local content, or create and restore manual backups. The app does not request your precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

2. Optional Plus account and cloud sync

If you choose Plus, Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication provide an account identifier and authorization information. The app does not ask Apple for your name or email address when you sign in.

Cloud sync is off until you explicitly enable it after reviewing a summary of the local records that will be uploaded. While Plus is active and sync is enabled, the service can synchronize your reading place, bookmarks, highlights, saved verse notes, reading preferences, saved Plus content and favorites, completed sermon notes and their completed review fields, saved weekly reflections, and any legacy guide completion retained from an earlier version. Unfinished sermon drafts, unfinished verse note drafts, feedback drafts, local suggestion history, raw search text, widget choices, and local diagnostic events are not included in cloud sync.

Synced content is associated with your account identifier so it can be returned to your signed in devices. The app sends changes in small records and uses technical revision, device, request, and timestamp values to prevent duplicates and resolve conflicts.

3. AI generated devotionals and verse insights

Plus lets you select a devotional theme or choose a random theme. The service selects Scripture from an approved local corpus and sends the selected passage, verified context, theme, and writing instructions to OpenAI to generate the devotional. It does not send sermon notes, sermon review responses, weekly reflections, verse notes, bookmarks, reading history, search text, email address, or name to OpenAI.

OpenAI input and output sharing is enabled only for the dedicated Doom Scroll project. This means the approved Scripture, server supplied context, bounded writing instructions, and generated commentary sent through that project may be used by OpenAI to improve its services. Model feedback sharing and evaluation and fine tuning data sharing are not enabled. Personal account information and private reading content are excluded from every generation request.

A daily devotional is generated only when requested. One daily result may be shared from a cache with other readers who choose the same theme on the same local date. The shared result is not labeled with your account. Your daily claim records the date and selected theme so a new daily choice cannot be taken repeatedly.

A verse insight sends the current canonical verse reference and server supplied Scripture context for a short explanation. Completed insights are matched to that exact verse, saved locally, and count toward the visible monthly allowance. An on demand devotional uses a purchased credit and creates a unique result. Generated content, limited usage counts, and request identifiers may be stored to deliver the feature, prevent duplicate charges, enforce limits, and investigate failures.

AI commentary may be incomplete or wrong. It is labeled separately from Scripture, does not provide a continuing chat, and is not medical, legal, financial, or professional spiritual advice.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes purchases, payment information, refunds, and subscription management. The developer does not receive your full payment card information.

RevenueCat receives an app account identifier, product and transaction identifiers, store, subscription status, expiration information, and related billing events. The service uses those events to grant access, restore purchases, add on demand credits, prevent duplicate credits, and remove access after expiration or refund.

Canceling stops renewal but Plus remains active through the paid period shown by Apple.

5. Local copies, backups, and sharing

Generated devotions and insights you open, sermon notes, completed reviews, and saved weekly reflections are saved locally. If Plus ends, local copies stay readable, editable where applicable, searchable, exportable, and available for manual backup on your device. If you subscribe again, eligible local data can synchronize to the service again.

A manual backup is readable JSON and can include private verse notes, drafts, sermon notes, sermon review responses, weekly reflections, local activity records, widget settings, saved references, reading places, legacy guide completion from an earlier version, feedback drafts, and preferences. It is not protected by an app specific password or encryption. Choose a destination you trust.

Sermon plain text and PDF exports are created on the device. Before export, the app shows a preview and lets you exclude personal thoughts, questions, application, prayer, and review responses. When you copy, export, or share content, the app sends it to the operating system clipboard or share sheet. Other apps, people, backup services, and destinations you choose handle the information under their own practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it, reset the app, or delete the app and its data. Offloading an iPhone app may retain its documents and data. Exported files and copies held by other services must be removed separately.

When Plus reaches its effective expiration or a verified refund or revocation is received, subscriber cloud content is scheduled for immediate removal. Automated retry jobs handle temporary deletion failures. Minimal billing events, credit receipts, security records, and deletion receipts may remain as needed to prevent fraud, provide purchase support, satisfy accounting duties, and demonstrate deletion.

You can export your current Plus cloud records from the Plus screen. You can also delete your Plus account and its current cloud copies after confirming your identity with Apple. Export, subscription management, and account deletion remain available while you are signed in even if Plus is no longer active. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription and does not remove local copies, so cancel the subscription separately in Apple subscription settings if you no longer want it to renew. Apple, RevenueCat, Firebase, OpenAI, Expo, and support email providers apply their own retention rules to information they process.

7. App updates, notifications, and support

Expo delivers compatible app updates and receives technical information needed for that service, including connection information, platform, runtime and update identifiers, a randomized installation identifier, and technical update failure information. Expo also provides aggregate app usage based on update requests.

Daily devotional and sermon review reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. A daily reminder does not generate a devotion in advance. Sermon review notifications use generic wording and do not include the sermon title, preacher, church, prayer, application, or note text. Turning reminders off cancels the app’s scheduled reminder.

The optional home screen widget reads a selected Scripture passage and theme colors from the app’s local shared container. It does not make network or AI requests and does not show account information, preacher or church names, sermon notes, prayer, or application text.

Feedback is optional. A draft stays on your device until you open your email app and send it. We receive your email address and anything you choose to send. Do not send private notes or backup files unless you intend to share them.

8. Service providers and security

The service uses Apple for purchases and sign in, RevenueCat for entitlement processing, Google Firebase for authentication and cloud infrastructure, OpenAI for bounded content generation, Expo for app updates and builds, and GitHub Pages for the public policy and support site.

Cloud requests use encrypted network connections. Server access checks, quotas, request identifiers, billing verification, and restricted database rules reduce misuse. No internet service is completely secure, and you should keep access to your device and Apple account protected.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. The effective date shows when this version took effect.

For privacy, support, export, or deletion questions, email doomscrollthebible@gmail.com.
