Help for your reading

Read the World English Bible Classic offline at your own pace. Plus adds optional sync, devotions, verse insights, structured sermon notes, weekly reflection, export, and a local Scripture widget without changing the complete free reader.

Contact the developer

Email doomscrollthebible@gmail.com for a problem, Scripture concern, purchase question, suggestion, or privacy request.

Publisher: Doom Scroll the Bible. Include the app version, phone model, operating system, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send private notes or backup files unless you intend to share them.

Subscriptions and purchases

Use Restore purchases in Doom Scroll Plus when a subscription is missing. Apple manages cancellation and refunds. Canceling stops renewal while access continues through the paid period shown by Apple.

Sermon notes and Saved Library

Sermon drafts save on the device as you write. Completed sermon notes sync only when Plus is active and private cloud sync is enabled. Existing local sermon notes, saved generated content, search, export, and backup remain available after Plus ends. Use Saved Library to reopen sermons, devotions, insights, weekly reflections, and favorites.

Cloud copies and account deletion

Cloud sync is off until you enable it. While Plus is active, enabled sync can keep your selected records available across devices. Delete Plus account removes current developer operated cloud copies after Apple identity confirmation but does not cancel Apple billing or remove local copies. Cancel the subscription separately in Apple subscription settings if you no longer want it to renew. At effective expiration or refund, cloud content is removed automatically and local copies remain.

Offline reading and updates

The Scripture corpus and fonts are bundled. Ordinary reading, sermon editing, local search, local review, export, and the selected Scripture widget work without a network. Store downloads, sync, generated content, and compatible app updates need internet access.

Back up before changing devices

Use Backup and restore to export a readable JSON file. It includes private notes, sermon notes, reviews, and local records, so choose a destination you trust. Sermon PDF and text export are human readable copies, not a complete app backup.

Report a text concern

Include the exact book, chapter and verse, the edition, and the wording you believe needs review. AI commentary is labeled separately from Scripture.
