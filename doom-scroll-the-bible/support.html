Help for your reading

Read the World English Bible Classic offline, at your own pace. Your saved reading stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

Contact the developer

Email mikelehotsky16@gmail.com for a problem, Scripture concern, suggestion or privacy request.

Publisher: Michael Lehotsky. Include the app version, phone model, operating system and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send private notes or backup files unless you intend to share them.

Getting started

Open the menu to choose a book, chapter or verse. Switch between verse and chapter reading at your current passage. Bookmarks, highlights and notes are in Your collection. Help remains available from the app.

Keep your place

Exploring another passage preserves your main reading place. Choose Back to my place to return, or Continue from here when you want to make the new passage your main place.

Offline reading and updates

The Scripture corpus and fonts are bundled. Ordinary reading works in airplane mode. Store downloads and compatible in-app updates need internet access. A downloaded app update offers a restart; update availability also depends on your store or testing channel.

Back up before changing devices

Use Backup & restore to export a readable JSON file. It includes private notes and local records, so choose a destination you trust. Restore shows a preview and replaces local data after confirmation, keeping one on-device recovery copy.

Reset or request deletion

Your data → Reset all local data removes active reader data and its local pre-restore recovery copy. Exported files and copies held by other apps or services must be deleted separately. There is no app account or developer cloud-sync database. For support-message deletion, contact the email above; records required by law may be retained.

Report a text concern

Include the exact book, chapter and verse, the edition, and the wording you believe needs review. The app uses World English Bible Classic; numbering and publisher notes can differ from other translations. Commentary guides are labeled separately from Scripture.
