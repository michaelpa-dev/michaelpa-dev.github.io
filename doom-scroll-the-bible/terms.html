Using the app

Doom Scroll the Bible provides Scripture reading tools and optional generated commentary for personal use. Scripture and generated commentary are presented separately. Generated content may be incomplete or wrong and should be considered thoughtfully.

Doom Scroll Plus

Plus is an automatically renewing subscription purchased through Apple. The price and billing period shown on the Apple purchase sheet control your purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple account after confirmation. The subscription renews unless you cancel through Apple at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple account subscription settings.

Plus includes optional cloud sync, bounded Cloud History and Recovery, one daily devotional choice for each local day, up to five new bounded verse insights per local day, and other features shown in the app. Unused daily devotional access and daily Insights do not roll over. Cloud recovery is temporary, does not replace manual backup, and is removed with other cloud content at effective expiration. Reasonable limits and temporary service pauses may protect reliability, safety, and cost.

Deleting a Plus account removes its developer operated cloud copies but does not cancel an Apple subscription or remove local copies on the device. Subscription cancellation is handled separately in Apple subscription settings.

On demand devotionals

An on demand devotional is a separate consumable purchase. One completed credit request produces one unique generated devotion. A credit is restored automatically when generation fails before a result is delivered. Consumable purchases may not be restorable by Apple after they are used.

Availability and changes

Cloud and AI features require internet access and may be unavailable during maintenance, provider incidents, safety review, or a configured cost limit. The complete offline Bible reader remains available without Plus. Features may change when necessary, but paid access will not be described in a misleading way.
